# Approved graphics catalog implementation audit

Source: `outputs/modernization-study-20261019/features.json`, all **47** entries in Image clarity (16), Display & performance (17), Lighting & content (14). This is a working-source audit, not a release claim. “Implemented” names concrete behavior; “partial” explicitly preserves the catalog's remaining work. Native D3D12 currently supports MSAA1 only; D3D11/SDL_GPU are the broad tested configurable paths. The implemented paths have CPU/GPU evidence; hardware, content and broader-platform limits remain explicit. Every backend does not support every option.

## Remaining validation and bounded scope

- All47 catalog entries have the concrete implementations or retained/scoped foundations listed above. Alpha-to-coverage has no eligible shipped cutout material; it is not a bat-wing fix. Material maps and mesh refinement need explicitly selected legal pack content; no replacement art is invented or bundled.
- D3D11/SDL_GPU provide broad effects. Native D3D12 provides vendor reconstruction/frame generation and ray shadows with its smaller capability set. HDR maps SDR art to scRGB; it does not recover clipped highlights or add HDR-authored emission. Physical HDR/VRR, mixed monitors and guns require suitable hardware.
- True Hor+ framing and matching aim/culling pass six chapter geometry fixtures. Extreme framing remains experimental because original scenery/staging were authored for4:3; broader playthrough/art tuning remains before a release default.
- Ray reflections/GI, Intel frame generation, Vulkan/Metal and non-Windows platforms remain separate unimplemented extensions. Scoped catalog implementations do not imply every vendor/API variant.
- Held Audio, Controls/accessibility and Player tools remain held. Voice and waiting-room chat are excluded. Only approved Home preview/shared rankings were added online; public ranking-service deployment remains separate from passing local implementation.
